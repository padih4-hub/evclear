Electric vehicles are no longer a niche choice in Australia. With over 11% of new car sales in early 2026 being electric, Australians are switching faster than ever. But an EV isn't the right choice for everyone — and knowing whether it suits your lifestyle before you buy will save you a lot of headaches.

## You're a great candidate for an EV if:

**You drive mostly in the city or suburbs.** Most Australians drive less than 50km per day — well within the range of even the most affordable EVs on the market. If your daily commute is predictable and you can charge at home overnight, an EV will almost certainly save you money.

**You have access to home charging.** Around 80% of EV charging happens at home overnight. If you have a garage, driveway, or dedicated parking spot, you can install a home wallbox charger and wake up every morning with a full charge. This eliminates range anxiety almost entirely.

**You're thinking long term.** EVs cost more upfront than equivalent petrol cars in most cases. But they cost significantly less to run — electricity is cheaper than petrol, servicing is simpler, and there are no oil changes. Over 3 to 5 years most EV owners come out ahead financially.

## An EV might not suit you yet if:

**You live in an apartment without dedicated parking or charging access.** This is the biggest barrier for Australian EV buyers right now. Without home charging you're relying entirely on public infrastructure which, while improving, is still inconsistent in some areas.

**You regularly drive long distances in regional or remote Australia.** Public fast charging networks are concentrated in cities and along major highways. If you frequently travel to remote areas, an EV requires more planning than a petrol car.

## The honest bottom line

For the majority of Australians living in cities and suburbs who own or rent with parking access — an EV makes complete financial and practical sense in 2026. The technology has matured, the charging network has improved dramatically, and the running cost savings are real.
