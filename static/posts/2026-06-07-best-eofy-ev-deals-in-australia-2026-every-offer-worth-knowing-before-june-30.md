**Which deals are actually worth acting on?**

Not all EOFY deals are created equal. Here is how to think about each one.

The BYD cashback is the most straightforward. $3,000 off at the time of purchase on some of the most popular EVs in Australia right now. No hoops to jump through, no finance requirement. If you were already looking at a Sealion 5, 6 or 8, this is a genuine saving.

The Renault Megane deal is compelling if you want something European and fully electric. Getting the GST equivalent off plus free on-road costs is effectively removing $7,000 to $9,000 from the total cost depending on the variant.

The Toyota bZ4X package at up to $12,500 in combined value sounds impressive but read the fine print -- most of that saving is in finance deposit contributions, which only applies if you take Toyota's finance product. If you are paying cash or using your own finance, the actual saving is smaller.

The Geely 0.88% finance rate is genuinely exceptional if you are financing. That is about as close to interest-free as you will find on any new car in Australia right now.

**The novated lease angle**

Here is something most buyers miss.

An EOFY cashback deal and a novated lease are not mutually exclusive. You can often stack them.

A $3,000 cashback on a BYD Sealion 5 combined with a novated lease means you are getting the cashback on top of the pre-tax dollar saving and zero FBT. For someone on a $90,000 salary, the combined saving over a three-year novated lease can be $15,000 or more compared to buying the same car outright with post-tax dollars.

The FBT exemption currently applies to eligible EVs under $91,387. All of the models in this article qualify. And with a government review flagged for mid-2027, locking in now protects your saving for the full lease term.

**What to do before June 30**

A few practical steps if you are serious about acting on any of these deals.

First, get your employer to confirm salary packaging availability if you are going down the novated lease path. That conversation takes time and you do not want to be rushing it in the last week of June.

Second, check stock availability at your local dealer. EOFY deals are subject to stock on hand, and popular models in popular colours can sell out quickly as June 30 approaches.

Third, understand what is actually being discounted. Cashback deals reduce the purchase price directly. Free charger deals are valuable but only if you were going to buy a charger anyway. Finance rate deals only matter if you are financing.

Fourth, do not let the deadline pressure you into the wrong car. A $3,000 saving on a car you do not really want is not a good deal. Use the EVClear calculator to make sure the numbers actually work for your situation first, then act.
