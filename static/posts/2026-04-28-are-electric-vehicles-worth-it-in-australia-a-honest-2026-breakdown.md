Let's be real — not everyone needs an EV right now. And that's okay. But if you're reading this, chances are you're at least curious. So let's cut through the noise and figure out if switching to electric actually makes sense for your life.

The good news? For most Australians living in cities and suburbs, it absolutely does. The bad news? There are still a few situations where you're better off waiting. Let's break it down.

## You're probably ready for an EV if:

**You drive mostly in the city or suburbs.** Most Aussies drive under 50km a day — an EV handles that before breakfast. If your commute is predictable and you can charge at home overnight, you'll almost certainly save money switching.

**You have somewhere to charge at home.** A garage, driveway, or dedicated parking spot is all you need. Plug in overnight, wake up full. Honestly? It's better than going to a petrol station. You'll never drive around looking for a servo again.

**You're thinking long term.** Yes, EVs cost more upfront. But between cheaper electricity, no oil changes, and less servicing — most owners save money within 3 to 5 years. The longer you keep it, the better the numbers look.

## You might want to hold off if:

**You live in an apartment with no charging access.** This is the number one barrier for Aussie EV buyers right now. Without home charging you're relying on public infrastructure — which is improving fast but still patchy in some areas. Worth solving before you buy.

**You regularly drive long distances through regional or remote Australia.** The charging network is getting better fast but it's not perfect yet outside major highways. Road trips require more planning than they would in a petrol car.

**You need a car this week.** Popular models still have wait times. If you're in a rush, your options might be limited. Plan ahead.

## The honest bottom line

If you live in a city or suburb, park at home, and drive a normal daily distance — an EV is almost certainly a smart move in 2026. The tech is solid, the savings are real, and you'll never queue at a servo again.
