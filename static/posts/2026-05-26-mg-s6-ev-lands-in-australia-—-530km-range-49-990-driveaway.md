### What You Get for the Money

Both variants are well equipped. Standard across the range includes:

Heated and ventilated front seats and heated steering wheel, 12.8-inch infotainment screen with wireless CarPlay and Android Auto, head-up display, rear seat recline and heating, 11-speaker audio system, 20-inch alloy wheels, full-width rear light bar, V2L capability at 3.3kW, and four regenerative braking levels including one-pedal mode.

The RWD model also gets an 86-litre frunk — useful extra storage that neither the Sealion 7 nor Zeekr 7X offers. The AWD has a slightly smaller 67-litre frunk.

Boot space is 581 litres expanding to 1,690 litres with the rear seats folded. Towing capacity is 750kg unbraked and 1,500kg braked.

### The Warranty

MG is offering a 7-year unlimited-kilometre warranty on the S6 EV — stronger than Tesla's 4-year warranty and matching BYD's 6-year. Buyers who service within the MG dealer network can extend this to 10 years or 250,000km through MG's Service Activated Extended Warranty program.

For buyers concerned about the long-term ownership experience with a Chinese brand, the warranty coverage is worth noting.

### Should You Buy One?

The MG S6 EV makes a compelling case on paper. The driveaway pricing undercuts every direct competitor, the range is the best in class at 530km, and the equipment level is strong.

The honest questions to ask before committing:

**Charging speed** — 144kW is fine for home charging but if you road trip frequently the Zeekr 7X at 360kW or Tesla's Supercharger network are meaningfully faster on long trips.

**Brand maturity** — MG has sold well in Australia but the S6 is a newer, larger model. Early adopter risk is real, though the warranty coverage mitigates it significantly.

**Dealer network** — MG has a growing dealer presence but it's not as extensive as Toyota or Hyundai. Check your nearest dealer before committing.

For buyers who primarily drive in the city and suburbs, charge at home overnight, and want maximum range and value at a drive-away price under $50,000 — the MG S6 EV is the strongest option in Australia right now.
