MG just launched a new small electric SUV in China priced at the equivalent of $20,500 Australian dollars. It's called the MG4X and it hasn't been confirmed for Australia — but if it arrives here at a similar price point, it would be the most disruptive EV launch this country has ever seen.

For context: the cheapest EV currently on sale in Australia is the BYD Atto 1 at $23,990 before on-road costs. The MG4X launched in China at less than that. And it's an SUV, not a hatchback.

Here's what we know, what we don't know, and whether Australian buyers should get excited.

- - -

### What Is the MG4X?

The MG4X is a small electric SUV sitting above the MG4 Urban hatchback — which launched in Australia in April 2026 from $31,990 drive-away — and shares design language with both the MG4 Urban and the MG S5 EV.

At 4,500mm long it's larger than the MG4 Urban and slots into the small SUV segment — competing with cars like the BYD Atto 2, GWM Ora, and the upcoming Leapmotor B10 if it comes to Australia.

The exterior follows MG's current design language with a large central screen interior, physical controls underneath, and a 10.25-inch digital instrument cluster.

### What Needs to Happen Before It Comes to Australia

MG hasn't confirmed Australian timing or pricing. Before getting too excited there are a few things worth noting.

Chinese launch prices rarely translate directly to Australian prices. By the time you add shipping, local compliance, dealer margins, and GST, Chinese EVs typically land in Australia at 30 to 50 per cent above their Chinese sticker price. That would put the MG4X at $27,000 to $31,000 in Australia — still extremely competitive if accurate.

Battery specs, range, and charging speed also haven't been confirmed. Range anxiety is still a real concern for many Australian buyers and a sub-30,000 EV with only 250km of real-world range would struggle to convert mainstream buyers regardless of price.

Export timing is flagged for late 2026 with Australia as a potential market. MG has been aggressive about expanding its Australian EV lineup — the MG S5, MG S6, and MG4 Urban all launched within the past 12 months. The MG4X fits the pattern.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**When is the MG4X coming to Australia?**

Not confirmed. MG has flagged export timing from late 2026. An Australian launch before mid-2027 would be optimistic.

**How does the MG4X compare to the BYD Atto 2?**

Both are small electric SUVs in a similar price bracket. The MG4X is longer at 4,500mm versus the Atto 2 at around 4,310mm. Full range and charging specs for the MG4X haven't been confirmed yet, making a direct comparison difficult at this stage.

**Does the MG4X qualify for the FBT exemption?**
