End of financial year is one of the best times to buy a new car in Australia. Dealers are pushing to hit targets, manufacturers are clearing stock, and finance offers get far more aggressive than normal.

For EV buyers in 2026 there's an added layer. The current EV FBT exemption is still fully available, which makes novated lease pricing particularly attractive right now. EOFY timing matters because many manufacturers are running aggressive promotions while the full exemption remains in place — creating one of the strongest combinations of discounted pricing, low finance rates, and fleet incentives the Australian EV market has seen.

### Geely EX5 — 0.88% Finance + Free Home Charger (Expires June 30)

Geely has one of the sharpest finance offers in the market right now. The EX5 Extended Range is available with 0.88% comparison rate finance, no deposit required, no establishment fee, and no repayment fees. Geely is also including a complimentary 7kW single-phase home charger on EX5 Extended Range purchases.

The EX5 starts from $40,990 and sits comfortably under both FBT thresholds. For buyers who want a capable family SUV at a competitive finance rate without stretching to a more expensive model, this is worth a serious look. Offer runs until June 30 for approved customers.

### BYD — 5% Novated Lease Discount on Full EV Range (Expires June 30)

BYD is offering a 5% novated lease discount across its full EV range through approved Fleet Management Organisations. Eligible models include the Atto 1, Atto 2, Atto 3, Dolphin, Seal, and Sealion 7.

BYD is also running separate cashback and finance offers across its PHEV range including the Shark 6, Sealion 5, Sealion 6, and Sealion 8 — but note those are plug-in hybrids, not full EVs, and the offers cannot be combined with other promotions.
