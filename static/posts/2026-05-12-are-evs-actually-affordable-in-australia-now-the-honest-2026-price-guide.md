### Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the cheapest EV in Australia in 2026?**

The BYD Atto 1 starts from $23,990 plus on-road costs, making it the most affordable new EV in Australia.

**Are EVs cheaper to run than petrol cars in Australia?**

Yes — significantly. Most EVs cost $4-6 per 100km to charge at home versus $16-22 per 100km for a petrol car. Over 15,000km a year that's roughly $2,000-2,500 in annual savings.

**Is it worth paying more for an EV upfront?**

For most Australians with home charging access, yes. The running cost savings typically offset the price premium within 2-3 years, and the novated lease tax benefit can eliminate the gap entirely.

**Which EV offers the best value under $40,000 in Australia?**

The MG4 Urban and BYD Atto 2 are the standout options under $40,000 — both offering genuine range, strong warranty coverage, and good build quality for the price.

**Will EV prices drop further in 2026?**

Yes — more models are arriving in the second half of 2026 and competition is driving prices down. However, waiting also means missing out on novated lease FBT benefits that are being wound back from April 2027.
