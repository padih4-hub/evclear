Two years ago, buying a new EV under $40,000 in Australia meant accepting serious compromises. Limited range. Basic interiors. Brands you'd never heard of with service networks you couldn't trust.

That's completely changed in 2026.

The sub-$40k EV market is now genuinely competitive. Real range. Real build quality. Real warranties. And prices that are giving traditional petrol hatchbacks a serious identity crisis.

Here's every EV worth considering under $40,000 in Australia right now — ranked honestly.

#### What we looked for

Before we get into the list, here's how we assessed each car. Range matters — but real-world Australian range, not the WLTP lab figure. DC fast charging speed matters for anyone who occasionally drives beyond their suburb. Warranty terms matter because these are still relatively new brands in Australia. And value matters — what do you actually get for the money?

With that said, let's get into it.

- - -

#### BYD Atto 1 Essential — from $23,990 drive-away

The cheapest new EV you can buy in Australia. Full stop.

And it's not a punishment. The Atto 1 Essential is a proper 4-seat city hatchback with 220km of real-world range, DC fast charging up to 60kW, and BYD's Blade battery technology — which has an excellent safety record.

At $23,990 drive-away it's now cheaper than a top-spec Toyota Corolla. That sentence would have sounded insane two years ago.

**Who it's for:** First-time EV buyers who want to try electric without a huge financial commitment. City dwellers. Second car buyers.

**The honest catch:** 220km real-world range is fine for daily commuting but leaves no buffer for anything beyond that. If you drive more than 80km on a regular day, step up to the Premium.

- - -

#### BYD Atto 1 Premium — from $27,990 drive-away

The pick of the Atto 1 range. A larger 43.2kWh battery lifts real-world range to around 310km, adds V2L capability — meaning you can plug appliances directly into the car — and brings DC charging from 10% to 80% in around 30 minutes.

For $4,000 more than the Essential, the Premium is the obvious choice for most buyers.

**Who it's for:** City and suburban drivers who want a genuinely affordable entry into EV ownership without feeling like they compromised.

- - -

#### BYD Dolphin Essential — from $33,792 drive-away

The Dolphin has been one of Australia's best-selling EVs for good reason. It's bigger than the Atto 1, seats five adults comfortably, and delivers around 340km of real-world range from a 44.9kWh battery.

The interior is a step up from the Atto 1 — more space, more refinement, more of a proper car feel. BYD's Blade battery and 8-year battery warranty give you confidence in the long term.

DC charging tops out at 60kW — slower than some rivals — but for a car that most owners charge at home overnight, that's rarely a dealbreaker.

**Who it's for:** Small families, couples, anyone who wants more space than a hatch but doesn't need a full SUV.

- - -

#### BYD Atto 2 — from $31,990 + on-road costs

Don't let the name confuse you — the Atto 2 isn't just a slightly better Atto 1. It's a small SUV with a proper 51kWh battery, 345km WLTP range, and 130kW DC fast charging — the fastest DC charging speed in this price bracket.

That 130kW charging speed is genuinely impressive at this price. It means on a road trip you're adding around 100km of range in under 15 minutes. For a sub-$35k car that's remarkable.

It also has V2L capability, a panoramic sunroof, and a more upright SUV body that gives you better visibility and more practical loading space than a hatch.

**Who it's for:** Anyone who wants small SUV practicality at hatchback prices, and values fast charging for occasional longer drives.

- - -

#### MG4 EV Urban 43kWh — from $31,990 drive-away

If value for money is your primary concern — and for most people at this budget it should be — the MG4 EV Urban is the benchmark.

For $31,990 drive-away you get a proper medium hatchback, 316km of real-world range, a full safety suite, and MG's 10-year/250,000km conditional warranty. That warranty alone sets it apart from everything else in this price range.

The 43kWh variant charges at up to 88kW DC — solid but not class-leading. The interior is functional rather than exciting. But if you want the most car, with the best coverage, for the least money — this is it.

**Who it's for:** Pragmatic buyers who want maximum value and the confidence of a long warranty. Families on a budget. Anyone buying their first EV who wants to minimise risk.

- - -

#### MG4 EV Urban 54kWh — from $34,990 drive-away

The long-range variant of the MG4 steps up to 405km WLTP range and 150kW DC fast charging — the fastest in this price range alongside the Atto 2.

At $34,990 drive-away with 150kW charging and 400km+ range, this is exceptional value. The extra $3,000 over the base MG4 buys you meaningfully more range and significantly faster charging — worth it for anyone who drives more than 15,000km per year or takes regular trips outside the city.

**Who it's for:** Buyers who want long-range confidence and fast charging without breaking $35,000. Hard to argue against at this price.

- - -

#### BYD Atto 3 — from $39,990 + on-road costs

The Atto 3 has been one of Australia's best-selling EVs for two years running and it's not hard to see why. Proper SUV body, 420km WLTP range, comfortable for five adults, and BYD's proven Blade battery technology.

The interior is genuinely premium at this price — better than anything MG offers and competitive with cars costing significantly more. The technology package is strong: wireless CarPlay, large touchscreen, V2L capability.

The one genuine weakness is DC charging speed at 88kW — slower than the MG4 Long Range and Atto 2 at a higher price point. Not a daily problem — but if you do a lot of highway driving it's worth factoring in.

**Who it's for:** Families who need SUV space and want the most established, proven EV in this price range. Buyers who prioritise interior quality and range over charging speed.

#### The honest ranking

If we had to pick one from this list for most Australian buyers:

**Best overall value:** MG4 EV Urban 54kWh — 405km range, 150kW charging, 10-year warranty, $34,990 drive-away. Hard to beat.

**Best entry point:** BYD Atto 1 Premium at $27,990 — proper range, V2L, 30-minute fast charge. An extraordinary amount of car for the money.

**Best SUV:** BYD Atto 3 — proven, spacious, premium interior. The safe choice if you need the space.

**Best charging speed for the price:** BYD Atto 2 — 130kW DC at under $35k is genuinely impressive.
