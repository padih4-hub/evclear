### What Makes a Novated Lease Different to a Car Loan

A novated lease isn't financing — it's a tax structure. Three parties are involved: you, your employer, and a finance company. Your employer leases the car on your behalf and the repayments come out of your salary before tax is calculated.

That does two things simultaneously. It lowers your taxable income — so you pay less income tax. And because eligible EVs are exempt from Fringe Benefits Tax, you avoid a cost that would normally add thousands per year to the arrangement.

The result is you end up paying significantly less for the same car than someone who bought it outright or on a standard car loan. For most Australian employees earning above $70,000, the saving over a 3-year lease on a mid-range EV runs between $13,000 and $25,000.

### The FBT Window — Why Timing Matters Right Now

The government has confirmed a phased wind-back of the EV FBT exemption. Here's what's locked in:

**Until 31 March 2027** — full exemption for all eligible EVs under the LCT threshold. Best possible outcome, nothing changes yet.

**1 April 2027 to 31 March 2029** — full exemption only for EVs under $75,000. Above that, it drops to a 25% discount.

**From 1 April 2029** — full exemption ends for everyone. All eligible EVs move to a 25% discount permanently.

Existing leases are expected to be grandfathered — meaning the rules that applied when you signed continue for the life of your lease.

The practical implication: if you sign a novated lease now on an EV under $75,000, you lock in the full exemption until 2029. If you're looking at something between $75,000 and the LCT threshold, you have until March 2027 before the benefit reduces.

Waiting isn't free. Every month you delay is another month paying full tax on your salary and full petrol prices on your current car.

### How to Pick the Right EV for a Novated Lease

Not every EV makes equal sense on a novated lease. Here's what to look for specifically:

**Stay under $75,000** — this keeps you in the full exemption zone past April 2027. Models like the BYD Seal ($52,990), Tesla Model 3 ($54,900), and Hyundai Ioniq 6 ($65,500-$85,500 depending on variant) all have options that sit comfortably under the threshold.

**Watch the accessories trap** — if you're buying close to the $91,387 LCT threshold and adding options before delivery, those extras count toward the LCT value. Push over the threshold and you permanently lose FBT eligibility on that vehicle. Always confirm the total drive-away price including all additions before signing.

**Consider running costs** — running costs like rego, insurance, servicing, and charging are all bundled into your pre-tax deduction on a novated lease. Lower running costs mean more of your pre-tax dollars go toward the car itself. EVs with lower servicing and charging costs give you a better deal overall.

### How to Get the Best Deal From Your Provider

This is where most people leave money on the table. The FBT exemption is the same regardless of which provider you use — but the fees, interest rates, and service levels vary significantly.

**Get at least 3 quotes** — this is non-negotiable. Providers know most employees don't shop around and price accordingly. A 30-minute comparison exercise can save you $3,000-$6,000 over a 3-year lease.

**Ask for total annual cost in dollars** — not percentages, not weekly repayment amounts. Total annual cost including all management fees, finance charges, and bundled services. That's the number you compare.

**Check what's actually included** — comprehensive insurance, servicing, tyres, charging costs. Ask for the PDS on the insurance policy before you sign. If a provider can't tell you exactly what's covered, that's a red flag.

**Don't get rushed** — a good provider won't pressure you to sign before you've had time to compare. Anyone creating urgency is trying to stop you from shopping around.

### The Residual — What Happens at End of Lease

At the end of your lease term you'll owe a residual — a lump sum set by the ATO as a percentage of the original vehicle value. For a 3-year lease it's around 46.88% of the purchase price.

On a $55,000 BYD Seal that's roughly $25,800. You have three options:

**Pay it out** — pay the residual and own the car outright.

**Refinance** — roll the residual into a new novated lease on a new car. This is what most people do — you effectively trade in and start again with a new vehicle and new lease.

**Hand it back** — return the car to the finance company. You walk away with nothing but you also owe nothing beyond the residual.

Most EV owners refinance into a new lease. The used EV market is still developing in Australia so values can be uncertain, but for popular models like the Tesla Model 3 and BYD Seal, residual values have held up better than expected.

### What to Watch Out For

**The reportable fringe benefit** — even though your EV is FBT-exempt, it still shows up on your income statement as a reportable fringe benefit. This doesn't change your income tax but it can affect HECS repayments, Medicare Levy Surcharge thresholds, and Centrelink eligibility. Talk to your accountant if any of those apply.

**Job change risk** — if you leave your employer mid-lease and your new employer doesn't offer salary packaging, the lease reverts to you personally. You keep the car but lose the pre-tax benefit for the remainder of the term. Factor this in if you're in a volatile industry or planning a career move.

**Cheap weekly repayments aren't always better** — some providers offer artificially low weekly repayments by setting a high residual. You pay less now but owe more at the end. Always check both the weekly repayment and the residual percentage together.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Is a novated lease worth it on an EV in Australia in 2026?**

For most employees earning above $70,000 with salary packaging access — yes. The combination of FBT exemption and pre-tax repayments creates savings that are very hard to replicate any other way.

**How long should I take a novated lease for?**

Most people choose 3 years. It balances manageable repayments, a reasonable residual, and the flexibility to upgrade to a new car at the end of term. 5-year leases give lower repayments but tie you to the car longer.

**Can I get a novated lease on any EV?**

No — the EV must be a full battery electric vehicle, first held and used after 1 July 2022, and under the LCT threshold ($91,387 for fuel-efficient vehicles in 2025-26) at first retail sale.

**What happens if petrol prices keep rising?**

Your EV running costs aren't affected by petrol prices. The charging cost savings over a petrol car become larger as petrol gets more expensive — which strengthens the case for switching.

**Can I include a home charger in a novated lease?**

The charger hardware and installation sit outside the novated lease. The ongoing electricity costs from charging can be bundled as a pre-tax expense.
