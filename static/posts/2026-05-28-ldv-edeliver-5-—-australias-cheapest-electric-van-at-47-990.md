Australia's electric van market just got a lot more accessible. LDV has opened order books for the eDeliver 5 with an introductory drive-away price of $47,990 for ABN holders — making it the cheapest electric van on sale in Australia right now, undercutting every rival including the Farizon V7E, Kia PV5, Volkswagen ID. Buzz Cargo, and Peugeot E-Expert.

The launch price runs until July 31, 2026 while stock lasts. First deliveries are expected in July 2026 — earlier than the previously planned 2027 timeline. LDV moved the timeline forward in response to surging diesel costs and what the company describes as a significant shift in small business buying behaviour.

EV sales in Australia hit a record 16.4 per cent market share in April 2026 — up from 6.6 per cent at the same point the previous year. The commercial van segment is catching up fast.

### Key Specs

The eDeliver 5 is built on a purpose-designed electric van platform rather than being converted from a petrol model — a meaningful advantage over some rivals.

All variants share the same electric motor and battery setup. V2L capability at 6.6kW is standard across the range — useful for tradespeople who want to run power tools directly from the van on a worksite without a generator.

The five-year, 160,000km vehicle warranty and eight-year, 250,000km battery warranty are among the strongest coverage packages available on any commercial EV in Australia.

### Why This Matters for Small Business

Australia's small business fleet market is one of the biggest untapped opportunities in the EV transition. Tradies, delivery drivers, and service businesses running diesel vans are paying record fuel costs — and the business case for switching to electric is now genuinely compelling for anyone who charges at their depot or home overnight.

At $47,990 drive-away, the eDeliver 5 SWB removes the biggest barrier to fleet electrification for small operators: purchase price. Add the 335km range which covers most metropolitan delivery routes comfortably, V2L capability for on-site power, and an 8-year battery warranty, and this is a serious commercial proposition.

The instant asset write-off for small businesses under $20 million turnover also means eligible businesses may be able to claim the full purchase price as a tax deduction in the year of purchase. Worth confirming with your accountant before ordering.

Add the instant asset write-off for eligible small businesses and the economics become even more compelling. For a business in the 30% company tax bracket, a full $47,990 write-off reduces tax by approximately $14,400 in the first year.

The 8-year, 250,000km battery warranty removes the biggest long-term risk concern for fleet operators.

For most small business operators running metropolitan routes — the eDeliver 5 is not just the cheapest electric van in Australia. On a total cost basis over five years, it's likely the cheapest van full stop.
