### How Fast Has BYD Grown in Australia?

To put the 100,000 milestone in context — BYD launched in Australia in 2022 with the Atto 3. In 2023 they were a curiosity. In 2024 they became a genuine contender. In 2026 they're now Australia's number one EV brand by volume, outselling Tesla in several months.

The Sealion 7 is currently Australia's best selling EV with sales up 342% year to date. The Seal, Atto 3, and Dolphin are all performing strongly across different price points.

### Why 30,000 More Vehicles Matters for Buyers

One of the biggest frustrations with popular EVs in Australia has been wait times. Tesla Model Y buyers are currently being told to wait until August for some variants. Supply constraints have been a genuine barrier to purchase.

BYD announcing 30,000 additional vehicles changes that equation. More supply means:

* Shorter wait times — potentially weeks rather than months
* Less dealer pressure to take whatever's available
* More negotiating room on price and inclusions
* More colour and variant options in stock

If you've been considering a BYD but put off by wait times, the supply situation is about to get significantly better.

### What This Means for Resale Values

More supply coming into the market is generally positive for buyers but worth thinking through for resale. When supply increases significantly, prices can soften slightly on the used market.

The honest take — this is unlikely to be a major issue for BYD models specifically. The brand is growing so fast that demand is keeping pace with supply. The Sealion 7 and Seal are both popular enough that used values should remain reasonable.

But it's worth factoring in if you're planning to trade out after 3 years. Check recent Carsales listings before committing to understand current used pricing trends.
