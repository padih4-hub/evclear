Not every EV is a smart choice on a novated lease. Some sit uncomfortably close to the LCT threshold. Others have running costs that eat into the tax saving. And some are just genuinely better value than others when you run the full numbers.

This list cuts through the noise. These are the best EVs for a novated lease in Australia in 2026 — picked specifically because they qualify for the FBT exemption, offer strong real-world value, and make the most of the tax structure a novated lease provides.

**What Makes an EV Good for a Novated Lease?**

Before the list, it's worth understanding what we're actually optimising for here — because it's not the same as picking the best EV overall.

For a novated lease specifically, the ideal car:

* Is well under the $91,387 LCT threshold — so there's no risk of losing the FBT exemption through accessories or price adjustments
* Has low running costs — because rego, insurance, servicing, and charging all come out of pre-tax salary, lower running costs mean more of your pre-tax dollars go toward the car itself
* Holds its value reasonably well — because you'll either pay a residual at the end or hand it back, and residual values matter
* Is under $75,000 — to keep the full FBT exemption beyond April 2027 when the rules change

If you're not across how the FBT exemption works or what the phased changes mean for your timing, read the [complete novated lease guide on EVClear](https://evclear.com.au/posts/2026-05-04-novated-lease-ev-australia-2026-how-it-works-what-you-save-and-what-to-watch-out-for) first.

**The Best EVs for a Novated Lease in Australia 2026**

**1. BYD Seal — From $62,900 drive away**

The pick of the bunch for most people considering a novated lease right now.

The Seal sits comfortably under both the $75k threshold and the LCT threshold, so you get the full FBT exemption now and you'll keep it after April 2027 when the rules tighten. It's a genuinely impressive car — 570km WLTP range on the long range variant, fast charging, a premium interior, and rear-wheel drive handling that punches well above its price point.

Running costs are low. The BYD battery platform is proven and servicing costs are reasonable compared to European alternatives at a similar price.

For someone earning $100,000 and leasing the Seal over 3 years, the estimated tax saving is $13,000 to $18,000. That's hard to beat at this price point.

**Why it works on a novated lease:** Strong value, well under the threshold, low running costs, excellent range.

**EVs to Be Careful With on a Novated Lease**

A few popular EVs that need a closer look before committing:

**Tesla Model Y Performance** — pushes close to the LCT threshold. Add accessories and you could lose the FBT exemption permanently. Check the final drive-away price carefully.

**Polestar 3** — sits above $75k which means from April 2027 you'll only get a 25% FBT discount instead of full exemption. Still beneficial but the maths changes noticeably.

**BMW iX and i7, Mercedes EQS, Porsche Taycan** — most variants exceed the LCT threshold entirely. No FBT exemption at all.

- - -

**The Accessories Trap — Worth Repeating**

If you're buying any EV close to the $91,387 LCT threshold and adding dealer accessories before delivery — tinted windows, upgraded wheels, floor mats, paint protection — those additions count toward the LCT value.

Push the total over the threshold and you permanently lose the FBT exemption on that vehicle. Always confirm the final drive-away price including every addition before you sign anything.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Which EV is best for a novated lease in Australia?** For most people the BYD Seal or Tesla Model 3 offer the best combination of value, range, and eligibility. The right answer depends on your budget and what you need the car to do.

**Does it matter which EV I choose for the FBT exemption?** Yes — the EV must be under the $91,387 LCT threshold at first retail sale. From April 2027, EVs over $75k lose the full exemption and drop to a 25% discount only.

**Can I add accessories to my novated lease EV?** Yes, but be careful. Accessories added before delivery count toward the LCT value and could push you over the threshold permanently. Always check the total drive-away price.

**What happens at the end of the novated lease?** You can pay the residual and own the car, refinance the residual into a new lease, or hand the car back.
