Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 May 2026

EVClear ("we", "us", "our") operates evclear.com.au. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Your email address when you subscribe to our newsletter or use the EV Savings Calculator
  • Information you provide when contacting us

We also collect certain information automatically when you visit our site, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on pages. This information is collected through standard web analytics tools.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send you EV news, guides, and updates you've subscribed to
  • Improve our website and content
  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Analyse how our site is used so we can make it better

Email Communications

When you provide your email address through our calculator or newsletter signup, we add you to our mailing list managed via Beehiiv. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email we send you.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve your experience and analyse site traffic. You can control cookie settings through your browser. By continuing to use our site, you consent to our use of cookies.

Third Party Services

We use the following third-party services which may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Netlify — website hosting and analytics
  • Beehiiv — email list management
  • Stripe — payment processing for digital products

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To make a request, contact us at hello@evclear.com.au.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@evclear.com.au.
