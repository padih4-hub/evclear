About EVClear

Built by an Aussie frustrated with confusing EV information

My name is Padi. I built EVClear because when I started researching EVs, I couldn't find a single Australian resource that gave me straight answers about what switching would actually cost me.

Every site was either written for the US market, full of sponsored content, or buried in jargon. I wanted real numbers — fuel savings, FBT exemptions, novated lease benefits — for my actual situation in Queensland.

So I built the calculator I wished existed, and started writing the guides I needed. EVClear is independent, ad-free, and built entirely around one question: what does an EV actually cost you?

No sponsored reviews. No dealer kickbacks. No affiliate spin.
